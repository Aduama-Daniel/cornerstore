# Product Rules

Last updated: 2026-06-13

## Publish Gate

A product must remain `inactive` until every required field is verified.

- Exact product identity and model number
- Source location or partner
- Source evidence date
- Current acquisition cost
- Current physical availability or reliable partner confirmation
- Original product photos or approved truthful media
- Selling price approved under `pricing-rules.md`
- Category and department
- Accurate specifications and claims
- Delivery feasibility
- Return and warranty treatment
- Owner responsible for rechecking availability

Unknown required information must be marked `MISSING`; it must not be guessed.

## Evidence Rules

- Keep a source URL, receipt, shelf-label photo, partner message, or visit note for every product.
- Record when the evidence was collected and by whom.
- Do not pair a shelf price with a product when the label-to-product relationship is ambiguous.
- Do not publish specifications copied from an unverified similar model.
- AI-generated images may support presentation only when they do not misrepresent the actual item, included accessories, color, size, or functionality.
- Record external research sources in the product record.

## Availability Rules

- Availability state must be one of: `unverified`, `confirmed`, `reserved`, `purchased`, `unavailable`, `stale`.
- Confirmation validity period: MISSING
- Partner response SLA: MISSING
- Automatically unpublish or pause stale products: MISSING
- Website stock must not imply owned inventory unless stock is actually held or reserved.
- For source-on-order products, label the fulfilment model clearly before launch.

## Catalog Quality Rules

- Use one canonical slug and model per distinct item.
- Variants must map to real source variants.
- Product title should include brand, useful differentiator, and model when known.
- Product descriptions should focus on verified customer-relevant facts.
- Do not use fashion-specific size, care, or return language for appliances and home goods.
- Duplicate and ambiguous source items belong in the exceptions log, not the live catalog.
