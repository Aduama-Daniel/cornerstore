# Cornerstore Business Brief

Last updated: 2026-06-13

## Business Model

Cornerstore is a Ghana-based e-commerce experiment. It lists products that can be sourced locally, including products available from retailers such as China Mall, applies a margin, promotes them online, confirms availability manually or through a partner, and delivers confirmed orders to customers.

## Current Operating Hypothesis

1. Discover locally obtainable products with visible customer demand.
2. Record enough source evidence to identify the exact item and current cost.
3. Calculate a selling price that covers sourcing, delivery operations, payment fees, returns risk, and margin.
4. Publish only products whose identity, price, claims, and fulfilment path are verified.
5. Promote selected products and measure the complete funnel.
6. Confirm source availability before promising fulfilment.
7. Purchase or reserve the item, deliver it, collect feedback, and feed the result into the next decision.

## Current Website Role

The existing website is a full-stack catalog and checkout system. It can serve as the customer storefront, catalog manager, order workspace, and initial measurement surface once its security, pricing integrity, inventory workflow, and analytics gaps are addressed.

## Near-Term Success Criteria

- Every active product has verified source, cost, availability status, and claims.
- Store and checkout totals are calculated from trusted server-side data.
- Admin and payment operations are access-controlled.
- Funnel events can be measured from product view through delivered order.
- A weekly decision process consistently promotes winners and pauses weak or unavailable products.
